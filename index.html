✨ Features

Everything you need to stay safe

MedScan works silently in the background, protecting you from medical misinformation as you browse the web.

🔍

Auto-Scan Pages

Automatically detects medical claims as you browse. No action needed — just install and forget.

❌

Instant Verdicts

Color-coded badges show you instantly: red for false, yellow for misleading, blue for unverified.

📚

Trusted Sources

Every verdict links to WHO, CDC, NIH, PubMed, and FDA — so you can verify for yourself.

Track how many claims you've encountered, how many were false, and your scan history.

🚀 How It Works

Up and running in 60 seconds

Three simple steps to protect yourself and your family from medical misinformation.

1

Download

Download MedScan from the repository. It's completely free and open source.

2

Install

Open Firefox, go to about:debugging, and click "Load Temporary Add-on" — takes less than 10 seconds.

3

Browse Safely

MedScan automatically scans every page you visit and flags suspicious medical claims.

👁️ See It In Action

Real-time detection

Here's how MedScan looks when it detects misinformation on a web page.

health-blog-fake.com/natural-cures

"Did you know that baking soda can cure cancer? Big pharma doesn't want you to know about this simple alkaline treatment that has helped thousands..."

❌ FALSE

No scientific evidence exists that baking soda cures cancer. The American Cancer Society warns against using baking soda as a cancer treatment. Tumors create their own acidic microenvironment that cannot be altered by diet.
